# Research and standards: context, not T36 validation

| Public doctrine topic | Reference | Supports | Does **not** support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence-linked state | [W3C PROV-O (2013)](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) | Formal model for provenance relationships | Any T36 reliability score |
| Gate and lineage metadata | [W3C PROV-DM (2013)](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/) | Provenance representations | Reduced hallucination due to the T36 fence |
| Immutable history | [Pro Git: Git Objects](https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2/Git-Internals-Git-Objects) | Content-addressed storage and audit concepts | Automatic correctness of committed data |
| External evaluation and risk | [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) | General measurement, governance and risk-management context | Crypto-market accuracy as a universal truth oracle |
| Social conformity concerns | [Bellina, De Marzo & Garcia, Conformity and Social Impact on AI Agents (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05384) | Observed conformity in studied agent configurations | A T36-specific or universal six-agent threshold |
| Multi-agent failure modes | [Chen et al., When and Why Does Multi-Agent Debate Fail and Does It Really Underperform? (2025, revised 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20963) | Debate protocol behavior and limits in its experiments | Validation of T36's architecture |

These are independent upstream works. They are **background/method references**, not borrowed receipts or endorsements. Please consult original publications and licenses before reuse.
