# Claim boundary

## What Part 4 supports

Within the tested homogeneous open-weight fleet and measurement framework:

1. The fleet does not provide evidence that the synthetic healthy-regime structure transfers cleanly to the real deliberation corpus.
2. The occupied band has essentially no practically separable split-vs-echo geometry under the pre-specified 0.15 criterion.
3. Synthetic calibration should therefore not be treated as universal calibration for real LLM fleets.

## What Part 4 does not support

Part 4 does **not** establish that:

- no real LLM fleet can occupy a healthy deliberative regime;
- the regime framework is universally false;
- a different independently calibrated representation could not reveal useful structure;
- healthy dynamics imply external correctness;
- changing fleet composition will solve the accessibility problem;
- any later controller is validated.

Those are separate empirical questions.

## Strongest alternative explanation

The simplest alternative is that the synthetic calibration does not transfer to real LLM deliberation at all.

That explanation must remain live. Calling the observed fleet the “wrong region” is a hypothesis about accessibility, not proof that the synthetic region exists as a transferable feature of real multi-agent systems.

## Public reading rule

**Failure to access a calibrated region and failure of the calibration to transfer can look similar observationally.**

Distinguishing those explanations requires new experiments, not reinterpretation of this result.
