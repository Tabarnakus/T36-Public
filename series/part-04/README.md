# X Series — Part 4: The Wrong Region?

Part 3 reported that the tested internal signatures did not separate market-vindicated from market-refuted consensus at the pre-specified magnitude.

Part 4 asks a different question:

> What if the real fleet was not operating in the dynamical region where the synthetic calibration suggested useful regime structure?

This evidence packet documents that question without claiming that the synthetic regime model transfers universally to real LLM deliberation.

## Reading rule

A regime that is visible in a controlled synthetic system is not automatically a regime occupied, or even cleanly separable, in a real LLM fleet.

**Calibration ≠ universality.**

The Part 4 result is therefore about **regime accessibility and transfer**, not external truth and not the performance of another fleet.

Later parts test separate questions. They are intentionally not disclosed here.
