# Fixed Evaluation Horizons

The market-grounded study evaluates recorded consensus decisions at three fixed future horizons:

- **H=1**
- **H=3**
- **H=5**

`H` denotes the predefined forward evaluation horizon in the experimental sequence. The important methodological point is that these horizons are fixed evaluation points rather than horizons selected after observing which one makes a prediction look best.

For each horizon, the internal deliberation is measured first and the external outcome is evaluated later.

Conceptually:

`state at decision time → H=1 / H=3 / H=5 future outcome → external comparison`

Using multiple horizons tests whether a relationship between deliberative dynamics and external validity is confined to one short timescale or persists across the tested forward horizons.

These horizons are part of the bounded market experiment. They should not be interpreted as universal cognitive timescales for multi-agent systems.
