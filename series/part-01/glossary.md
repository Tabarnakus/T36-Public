# Part 1 Glossary

This glossary translates T36 terminology into plain language. Formal measurements are introduced in later parts.

### Agent
One AI model instance participating in the deliberation.

### Fleet
The group of agents participating in the same experimental deliberation.

### Multi-agent deliberation
The process in which multiple agents produce and, depending on the experimental condition, respond to reasoning about the same bounded problem.

### Consensus
Agreement among the fleet on the directional decision being evaluated. Consensus is **not** treated as proof of correctness.

### External adjudicator
A source outside the deliberating fleet used to determine the later outcome. In this experiment, that source is the realized market movement.

### External validity
Whether the recorded consensus is supported or contradicted by the later external outcome under the experiment's adjudication rule.

### Vindicated / refuted
Plain-language labels used in the X narrative for a recorded consensus that agrees / disagrees with the later externally observed directional outcome.

### Horizon (`H`)
A predefined future evaluation point. The study reports H=1, H=3 and H=5.

### Deliberative dynamics
Measurable changes in the agents and their relationships during reasoning. Part 2 introduces the principal T36 observables used to describe these dynamics.

---

## Reading rule for the series

T36 deliberately separates three ideas that are easy to blur together:

**agreement ≠ healthy dynamics ≠ truth**

The X series follows the experiments that led to that distinction.
