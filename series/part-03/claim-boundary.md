# Claim Boundary

Part 3 supports a bounded null result:

> **Across the instruments and horizons tested, no internal signature reached the pre-specified separation criterion between externally vindicated and externally refuted consensus.**

That is narrower than saying that no useful internal signature can ever exist.

## What the result supports

- The tested concept-space and response-space instruments did not meet the locked silhouette criterion.
- The null was not confined to a single adjudication horizon.
- Most tested instruments are inconsistent with a separation as large as the pre-specified target in this sample.
- The trajectory descriptor's AUC is worth replication, but not elevation into a positive finding.

## What the result does not support

- It does not prove that deliberative dynamics contain zero information under every possible representation or instrument.
- It does not prove that another domain, fleet, embedder, or larger corpus would produce the same result.
- It does not establish why the separation failed.
- It does not distinguish among competing explanations such as correlated priors, noisy external outcomes, inadequate instrumentation, or genuinely absent internal validity signal.

The point of the null result is therefore not that the search is over.

It is that the first tested instruments did **not** cross the evidential line fixed before the result was known.
