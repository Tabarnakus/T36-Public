# Claim boundary

## Supported

- In the **six recorded paired market windows**, the NEW three-model roster had a higher native directional split rate than the OLD three-model roster.
- The arithmetic contrast is **6/6**, mean **+14.6 percentage points**.
- Exact signed-rank p = **0.03125**, **conditional** on pairwise sign-exchangeability/independence assumptions that have not been independently audited.
- The observed comparison associates a **change in tested fleet composition** with a **change in recorded deliberative form**.

## Not supported

- That the historical comparison isolates a homogeneous-to-heterogeneous treatment. **Both recorded rosters contain different model families.**
- That heterogeneous fleets *in general* disagree more, reason better or are more truthful.
- That the effect was caused uniquely by model lineage rather than scale, provider, capability, response priors or other changes.
- That the six windows are independent or represent a general population of tasks.
- That the raw source agent events, model calls or classification rules have been independently replicated.
- That increased disagreement improves consensus-following accuracy or prevents echo chambers.

The distinction between statistical reanalysis and experimental replication is documented in [Receipt 01](../../receipts/paired-fleet-disagreement/README.md).
