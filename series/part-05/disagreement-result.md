# Recorded disagreement: NEW versus OLD

Across six paired historical market windows, the **NEW** three-model fleet recorded more native directional splits than the **OLD** three-model fleet in **all six windows**.

| Quantity | Historical aggregate result |
|---|---:|
| Matched market windows | **6** |
| Windows with NEW > OLD split rate | **6/6** |
| Mean paired difference, NEW minus OLD | **+14.6 percentage points** |
| Wilcoxon exact two-sided p, conditional on its assumptions | **0.03125** |

The two historical rosters both use multiple model families. They should **not** be presented as a controlled homogeneous/heterogeneous contrast.

**This establishes an observed shift in recorded split rates for the tested fleet compositions.** It does not establish causality for a specific component of fleet diversity, improved accuracy or prevention of echo chambers. Independence or non-overlap of the six market windows has not been independently verified.
