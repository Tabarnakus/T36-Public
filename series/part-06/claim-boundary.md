# Claim boundary

## Supported by the published summaries

1. Part 5 reported higher native split rates for NEW than OLD in six matched windows.
2. Part 6 reported pooled market consensus-following accuracy **below 50%** for both rosters at three horizons.
3. The summaries therefore show that an observed difference in deliberative form **does not establish** improved external accuracy.

## Unsupported

- Calling the two historical rosters "homogeneous" and "heterogeneous" as a controlled treatment: both contain different model families.
- Claiming statistically demonstrated chance-level performance without the relevant confidence analysis.
- Treating Part 6's pooled outcome observations as the **same six paired windows** used in Part 5.
- Identifying a universal causal role for diversity, model size or vendor.
- Generalizing the results to unrelated reasoning domains or asserting that any controller has been validated.

The raw data and execution logs are not contained in the public repository. Keep these limitations visible, rather than upgrading a documented analysis to a replicated experiment.
